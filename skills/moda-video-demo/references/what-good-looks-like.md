# What a good product demo does

Measured from three published Gamma demos, watched end to end by a video model
rather than sampled as frames. Numbers are from the videos, not from taste.

| | Mars deck (119s) | Connectors (72s) | Glean (29s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| music | from 0s, continuous | from 0s, continuous | from 0s, continuous |
| voiceover | none | none | none |
| captions **not** part of the app | none | 4 total | 2 total |
| zoom count | 2 | 19 | 1 |
| zoom hold | brief | 0.5–1.0s | 0.5s |
| out of a zoom | eases back | **cuts** | **cuts** |
| opens on | product, 0s | product, 0s | 7s title card |
| ends on | logo + CTA, 9s | logo + CTA, 6s | logo + CTA, 5s |

## Unanimous — treat as the default

1. **Music from the first frame, continuous, never ducking.** All three. It is
   what carries the pacing; the second video's analysis says so outright.
2. **No voiceover.** All three. Value is carried by the product's own on-screen
   text — a chat reply, a generated slide — not by a narrator.
3. **No per-step captions.** Zero, four, and two *in the whole video*, and never
   one per interaction. Where they exist they are bare white centred text with no
   plate, at the open, at one transition, and at the close.
4. **Zoom holds about half a second and CUTS out.** Not an ease back to wide. The
   19-zoom video proves frequency is not the problem — dwell is.
5. **A logo and CTA card at the end.** All three, 5–9 seconds.

## Varies — a per-demo decision, not a rule

- **Zoom count**: 1, 2, 19. Driven by how much there is to point at.
- **An intro card**: two open cold on the product; one runs a 7-second title.
- **Cursor treatment**: one has a click ripple, one is explicitly plain.
- **Music tempo**: upbeat electronic for a feature tour, lo-fi for a quiet
  integration demo. Matched to the content.

## The caveat that matters most

**All three are the same company, and all three are the same GENRE.** Each shows
an *outcome* — type a prompt, a deck appears — where the product does the work
centre-screen and nothing needs to tell you where to look.

A navigation demo is a different thing. "Find the connector settings three levels
down" is a task where *where to click* is the content, which is exactly what
tutorial screencasts caption and marketing demos do not. Do not read "no captions"
as a law until there is a reference that attempts a navigation demo without them.

So: ask which is being made. An outcome demo wants music, no captions, and a hard
cut out of every zoom. A navigation demo probably still wants its captions, and
the rest of the list still applies.
